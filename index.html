The proposed approach

Space gives context.
You give it meaning.

juxtacrop would connect Earth observations with soil information, crop history and knowledge from the farmer.

GPM / IMERGRainfall context↗Opens in a new tab.POWERClimate context↗Opens in a new tab.SMAPSurface soil moisture↗Opens in a new tab.HLSVegetation over time↗Opens in a new tab.

Proposed integrations, not live data. IMERG and SMAP provide regional context, not measurements for every small parcel. HLS quality depends on resolution and clouds. POWER includes observations and models. Soil context: USDA soil surveys ↗.
